How it is measured

Net WPM uses currently correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes. Raw WPM uses all inserted keystrokes ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes. Accuracy is based on correct versus mistyped inserted keystrokes, so correcting an error does not erase the attempt.
